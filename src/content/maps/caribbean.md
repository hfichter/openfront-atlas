## The Geography

Caribbean stretches from Florida and the Yucatán to the Lesser Antilles and the northern coast of South America. Across its wide 3200×1808 frame, Cuba, Hispaniola, Puerto Rico and dozens of smaller islands form a long arc through open water. Land accounts for just 10% of the map, while 34 nations crowd its coasts and archipelagos.

## The History

Indigenous Caribbean societies were transformed after European arrival in 1492. Spanish, British, French and Dutch empires then competed for islands and plantation wealth built on enslaved labour; revolutions and independence movements, beginning spectacularly in Haiti, reshaped the region.

## The Battlefield

This is one of OpenFront's most naval battlefields. Large islands offer the safest early economies, but no position remains isolated once fleets appear. The Antillean chain works as a series of stepping stones, and control of Cuba or Hispaniola opens several attack directions. Expand selectively, defend ports and avoid scattering forces across too many tiny islands.
