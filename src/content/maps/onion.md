## The Map

Onion is a tiny 512×512 arcade arena shaped as nested layers around a central core. Three factions—the outer confederation, an enclave and the inner tribe—occupy a field that is 80% land. Concentric barriers turn a simple silhouette into a sequence of increasingly intimate fronts.

## The Battlefield

Each ring limits access to the next, so gateways matter more than total territory. The outside has room but a long perimeter; the centre has short internal routes but little space to absorb pressure. Breaking one layer can expose the defender behind it, yet an unsupported breach is easy to close. Concentrate strength at a single opening and protect your route between rings.
