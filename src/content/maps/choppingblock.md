## The Map

Chopping Block is a square 1616×1616 arcade maze divided into four mirrored quadrants. Its checkerboard corridors, boxed chambers and narrow cuts hold 32 named starts and 57% land. The geometry is artificial and highly legible: every opening is an invitation, and every wall can turn a short distance into a long route.

## The Battlefield

The map is designed for four-way pressure. Local control comes from chamber exits, while the central crossings decide whether forces can rotate between quadrants. A player who expands without securing the next doorway can be sealed into a pocket. Clear one block at a time, hold the junctions and keep a reserve near the centre for sudden breakthroughs.
