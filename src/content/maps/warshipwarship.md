## The Map

Warship Warship is a wide 3000×1396 arcade arena shaped around two opposing pixel-art vessels. Ten pirate-themed starts occupy just 27% land, with additional realms ready to populate the mirrored decks and surrounding waters. The playful design turns the ships themselves into terrain and gives the battlefield a clear two-sided structure.

## The Battlefield

Each vessel offers a compact home area before pressure crosses the central gap. Narrow hull sections and deck openings create predictable chokepoints, while naval routes threaten the outside edges. A direct rush can work, but losing control of your own ship leaves little depth for recovery. Clear the home deck, hold the central approaches and coordinate crossings rather than feeding forces through one opening.
