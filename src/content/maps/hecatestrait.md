## The Geography

Hecate Strait separates Haida Gwaii from the mainland coast of British Columbia. The map is a long, irregular sweep of islands, fjords, inlets, and mountain-backed shoreline: open water in the west, narrow passages and river valleys toward the mainland. Haida Gwaii forms a distinct western theatre, while the central and northern coast is broken into many defensible approaches.

## The History

These waters are part of the homelands of the Haida and of many Indigenous nations on the central and northern coast, including Gitxaala, Haisla, Gitga’at, Kitasoo, Heiltsuk, Wuikinuxv, Nuxalk, and neighbouring Tsimshian and Carrier communities. The settlements represented on the map are living communities, not simply historical labels. The modern chart name, Hecate Strait, sits within a much older network of Indigenous sea routes, villages, and place-names.

## The Battlefield

### Terrain Overview

Hecate Strait is a map of separated theatres. Water divides the board, but the maze of channels means that a rival can often approach from more than one direction. Island interiors offer depth; mainland inlets create strong fronts that can become traps when their entrances are lost.

### Best Spawns

- **Island interiors** - room to expand behind a protected shoreline.
- **Channel mouths** - control over a passage can connect several coastal fronts.
- **Mainland valleys** - useful staging ground when you can secure the approaches.

### Avoid

- **Exposed outer coasts** - a long shoreline can become an easy front for several rivals.
- **Dead-end inlets** - water may shield one flank while leaving no route of retreat.
- **Neglecting the strait** - a player who controls the central crossings can choose where pressure lands.

### Strategic Insights

Treat Hecate Strait as a network rather than a single front. Establish a secure base, then hold the channel mouths that connect your islands and mainland positions. Ports and narrow crossings are more valuable than a quick grab of empty coastline; they let you shift forces before an isolated settlement is surrounded.

## Fun Facts

- Hecate Strait lies between Haida Gwaii and the mainland coast of British Columbia.
- The map brings together communities from Haida Gwaii and the central and northern coast.
- Its fjords and island passages make geography itself the main source of chokepoints.
