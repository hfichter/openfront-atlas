## The Map

Labyrinth is a 1360×1360 arcade board built from a repeating grid of walls, rooms and offset doorways. Its 26 alphabetically named starts sit on a land-heavy field of 82%, but the walls make usable space far scarcer than that figure suggests. Nearby territory may require a long route through several junctions.

## The Battlefield

Path knowledge matters more than raw distance. Doorways become chokepoints, loops create surprise flanks and closed-looking pockets may connect elsewhere. Expanding blindly can leave a thin supply trail through a single gate. Claim intersections, scout routes before committing and keep enough strength behind you to stop an opponent from sealing the corridor home.
